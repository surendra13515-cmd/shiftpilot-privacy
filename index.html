Shift Pilot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Shift Pilot ("the App") is developed by SP Tech Labs. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how the app collects, stores, and uses your data.

Information We Collect

  • User profile information entered by you (Name, Company, Department, Punching Code, etc.).
  • Shift schedules.
  • Working hours.
  • Salary calculations.
  • Overtime (OT) hours entered by the user.
  • Overtime (OT) calculation records and analysis data.
  • Notes created by the user.
  • App settings and preferences.

How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate salary estimates.
  • To calculate overtime (OT) earnings.
  • To display overtime summaries and dashboard analytics.
  • To generate reports.
  • To display shift history.
  • To improve user experience.

Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device. Shift Pilot does not upload or share your personal information with external servers unless you choose to export or back up your data.

PDF Export

Shift Pilot allows users to export their shift history and salary records as PDF files.

Exported PDF files are generated locally on your device and are saved only to the location you choose. We do not upload, store, access, or share your exported PDF files on our servers.

The exported PDF may include information such as shift dates, shift names, and salary details based on the data available in the app at the time of export.

All shift records, salary information, and exported PDF reports remain on your device unless you choose to share or back them up yourself.

JSON Backup & Restore

The app allows users to export and restore their own data using JSON backup files. These files remain under the user's control.

Third-Party Services

Shift Pilot may use the following third-party services where required for app functionality:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (for displaying advertisements in the free version of the app)

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Shift Pilot is not intended for children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
